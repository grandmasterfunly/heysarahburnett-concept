Meet your guide

Hi, I'm Sarah. I help you heal from the roots up.

I'm a Licensed Mental Health Counselor, Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy guide, Hypnotherapist, Psychedelic Somatic Facilitator and Yoga Teacher.

Over the years I've found that the fastest route to real transformation is working directly with the subconscious mind, where our deepest wounds and limiting beliefs are stored. With a blend of hypnotherapy, parts work (IFS), NLP, somatic healing and psychedelic integration, I help growth-oriented seekers break free from mental loops and feel empowered to live in freedom, not fear, without years of talk therapy.

I come from a line of intuitives, from my Nana Suni to my mom and co-host Petey. I spent three years learning from monks, healers and yogis across Thailand, Bali, India, Australia and New Zealand, then came home to Orlando to open my practice. After the Pulse tragedy in 2016, I made it my mission to master the most effective trauma techniques for my community. Many of my clients are LGBTQ+.

    What a session looks like

    Six steps, from first hello to integration.

      With Sarah

      A free discovery call

      Twenty minutes to talk through your goals, answer your questions and see whether KAP or integration coaching is a good fit. No health details are collected through this website. We talk about those privately.

      With a licensed prescriber

      Medical evaluation

      Sarah doesn't prescribe or dose medication. An independent, licensed medical provider screens your health history and medications and decides whether ketamine is appropriate and safe for you.

      With Sarah

      Intention & setting

      We prepare your (mind)set and setting: a clear intention, a calm and comfortable space, a curated music journey, and support people in place, because these shape the experience.

      Guided

      Settle in & meditate

      You take the medicine exactly as your prescriber directs while Sarah leads a short guided meditation to help your body settle and your nervous system feel safe.

      Held space

      The music journey

      Eye mask on, headphones on, for roughly 75–90 minutes. Many people describe being the calm observer of their own story. Every journey is different, and you're supported the whole time.

      With Sarah

      Nourish & integrate

      Rest and a nourishing meal, then an integration session to turn insights into everyday practice. That's where the lasting change happens.

      Important to know

      • Ketamine is FDA-approved as an anesthetic. Its use for mental health is off-label. KAP isn't a cure, and individual results vary.
      • Sarah does not prescribe or dose medication. A licensed medical provider handles all eligibility, screening and prescribing decisions.
      • Talk with a medical provider first, and be especially cautious, if you are pregnant, have uncontrolled heart or blood-pressure conditions, active mania, a history of ketamine misuse, or heavy alcohol or benzodiazepine use that may need supervised detox.
      • Never stop or change medications without your prescriber. Experiences shared in the episode are personal stories, not guarantees.

      In crisis or thinking about suicide? Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency.

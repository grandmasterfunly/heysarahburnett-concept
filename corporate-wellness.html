About Sarah

Confidence and clarity through mind and body

Sarah Burnett is a licensed mental health counselor, transformational mindset coach, yoga instructor, meditation teacher, retreat facilitator, and co-host of the Therapy Unfiltered with Mom & Me podcast. Sarah specializes in leadership and future success coaching, guiding corporate professionals and entrepreneurs into a place of confidence and clarity through mind and body integration.

Sarah utilizes a combination of guided visualizations, breath work, and mindfulness techniques to remove self-limiting beliefs and doubts that can inhibit professionals from showing up the way they want to, especially in situations that require mental agility and clear communication.

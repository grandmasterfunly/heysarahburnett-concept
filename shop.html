How I got hooked

Rishikesh, 2013

I was introduced to dōTERRA oils in 2013 while completing my yoga teacher training in Rishikesh, India, and I am forever grateful I had DigestZen for my "Delhi Belly" and Deep Blue for my sore muscles. And just like that, I was hooked!

It has been a game changer for me to lower my toxic burden in my home and shift my mood through breathwork. Not only did I love taking healing into my own hands, but I felt safe knowing that these are Certified Pure Therapeutic Grade oils, which means they go through rigorous third-party testing. Be sure to read labels because not all oils are created equal.

Plants have been used as medicine for thousands of years. Bottling the healing power of plants makes it easily accessible for everyday wellness. Pro-tip: always carry your essential oils with you!

My story

A soul-infused path of healing and wisdom

For as long as I can remember I have always been surrounded by angels, felt that I’ve lived multiple lifetimes, understood that my body stores unresolved feelings and traumatic memories, knew that I can manifest and that I chose my siblings, parents, and friends as part of my soul contract for healing and growth.

My Nana Suni was an intuitive who believed in the power of our mindset, meditation, dream interpretation, crystals, spirits, creative energy and so much more! She infused her deep wisdom into my mother and thus, into me. I soaked up all that I could from Nana until the age of 12 when she transitioned. Even though she’s on the other side, I feel her when the lights flicker, the smell of Habanita perfume wafts across my nose, or this warm presence flows throughout my body. She’s my guardian angel and has worked some magic for me behind the scenes. Thank you forever, Nana.

My mom carried on the spiritual knowledge she inherited from Nana to our family growing up. I remember having sleepovers with friends, and in the morning we would all gather around the breakfast table to have mom interpret our dreams. I remember going into the bathroom with her, dimming the lights, and looking into the mirror so she could teach me how to read my aura. We spent many Saturdays out at the beach soaking up nature and resetting in the sea. Just like Nana, mom is an intuitive, but she started out as a traditional Marriage & Family Therapist before finding her way back to Spirit through channeling messages and past-life regression.

I guess you could say that being intuitive was in my genes. I’ve always had this strong felt sense in my body; a knowing or a wisdom that I could tap into. Sometimes it shows up as chills, and other times it's a warm soothing feeling or a cold jerk of my body. However it shows up, I’ve learned to listen to it and trust that it is presenting for my highest good. This intuitive knowing or gut instinct led me safely around the world for 3 years, from exploring the mountains of Thailand meditating with Monks to living off-grid on an island near Cambodia to falling in love with sacred Balinese traditions to living in a van as I traveled the whole of Australia. And then so beautifully, I was guided to India for my yoga teaching training and Reiki certification, then back to Bali to drop into the ritualistic wisdom of Transcendental Meditation (TM) developed by Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, and finally onto New Zealand for a solo adventure allowing me to tap deeper into meditation, yoga and expansiveness. With little access to the internet and only an old-school flip phone, I recognize how my intuitive self and my Guardian Angel were my guiding light.

When I returned to the states, I found my way back to my hometown of Orlando to start my counseling private practice with my mom. I started my practice as a play therapist for children 2 to 12-years old. And I’ve continued to see a handful of younger kids throughout the years; it is one of my favorite joys in life! Children fully live in their subconscious mind and with that, have the ability to shift, heal and adapt their mindset at a much quicker speed than us adults who can feel hesitant of new ways of being and perhaps resistant to change. Since children are so available for these new learnings, I’ve moved into Parent Coaching where I empower parents with tangible tools so they can create a home full of compassion, respect and beautiful connection.

As the practice grew into seeing a wide range of clients from young adults to retired professionals, I recognized the need to expand past the limitations of talk therapy. I have found some of the most powerful healing modalities over the years to help guide my clients out of their darkness and into a place of inner knowing and safety within themselves. I specialize in working with the LGBTQ+ community among teenagers and adults, and after the Pulse shooting in Orlando in 2016 it became my mission to learn the best trauma therapy techniques.

My approach

How I work

Trauma-informed techniques

Certified in Reconsolidation of Traumatic Memories (RTM) and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART), I employ gentle memory reconsolidation techniques and bi-lateral brain stimulation to effectively alleviate post-traumatic stress symptoms, catering to diverse experiences such as domestic violence, grief, and phobias.

Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP)

NLP is a foundational tool woven throughout all of my coaching because it’s an extremely gentle way to tap into the subconscious mind. I use guided imagery and compassionate language to allow clients to clear past pains, connect to the present, and project future successes.

Hypnotherapy & past-life regression

Certified in Hypnotherapy and Past-Life Regression Therapy, I guide clients through age regression into childhood and exploration of past lives. This transformative journey fosters deep emotional healing on a holistic level, empowering individuals to discover answers within themselves.

Relief & empowerment

Through the transformation of limiting beliefs and the release of past traumas and negative emotions, clients experience profound relief. Witnessing faces fill with vibrance, shoulders softening, and a brightened aura, I guide individuals to manifest positive connections in mind, body, and spirit.

Connection to the subconscious

Recognizing the power of the subconscious mind, I provide a safe space for clients to explore and transform. The goal is to offer a range of healing modalities, facilitating a connection with oneself, nature, and the universe, fostering a life enveloped with joy, peace, resilience, truth, and love.

Personalized coaching

As your coach, I invite you to embark on a transformative journey, offering personalized sessions tailored to your unique needs. If you feel the intuitive call, a discovery call awaits, marking the beginning of your path towards self-discovery and transformational healing.

Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Everyone deserves access to healing. This site is designed and tested to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, and Level AAA where practical.

What we've done

  • Built mobile-first with semantic HTML, landmarks, a skip link, and a logical heading order.
  • Full keyboard support with clearly visible focus indicators, plus touch targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels.
  • Body text contrast meets AAA (7:1 or higher). All other text meets at least AA.
  • Captions on every video and a full, searchable transcript for the featured podcast episode. Background videos are silent and decorative, and they can be paused.
  • Respects your device's "reduce motion" setting. There's also a "Reduce motion" button in the footer that turns off animation and autoplay.
  • No accessibility overlays. Accessibility is built into the code itself.
  • Automated testing (axe-core and pa11y) runs on every change, along with manual keyboard and screen-reader checks.

What is a Soul Coach?

Are you looking for a certified guide on your spiritual journey?

A Soul Coach is a professional who has taken the Beginner and Advanced Soul Learner Courses as well as the 3-month Soul Coach Training Program. Coaches are directly trained by Petey Silveira and specialize in guiding and supporting individuals on their spiritual journeys, helping them navigate and understand their soul lessons, purpose, and personal growth.

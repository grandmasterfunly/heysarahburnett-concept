About this service

Parents are the agents of change

With a toolbox full of new tangible skills, you'll feel the family dynamic completely shift.

I love working with parents to empower the entire family unit. A household full of yelling, arguments and disregard for others will transform into a warm, loving home where all members of the family feel respected and supported.

As a Parent Coach, I work directly with the parents to ensure you master the limit-setting language and feel equipped when your child flips their lid and becomes defiant. My approach engages the entire family system to practice healthy coping skills, utilize constructive communication for conflict resolution, reframe negative self-talk, and role model present moment focus, impulse control and mutual respect. The more involved parents, caregivers and mentors become, the quicker the transformation unfolds and homeostasis returns.

Empowered Parent Coaching is suitable for parents with elementary, middle and even high-school aged children. The framework stays the same; however, the language expands and grows up alongside your child.

I know you have a never-ending to do list and barely enough time to squeeze in time for yourself! The Empowered Parenting Coaching method is structured in 30-minute laser coaching sessions so you can grab the tools you need without disrupting your day.
